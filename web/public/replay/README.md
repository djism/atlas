# Replay data (derived from KITTI)

These files are derived from the KITTI Vision Benchmark Suite, raw drives
2011_09_30_drive_0027 (odometry sequence 07) and 2011_09_30_drive_0016
(sequence 04), and its odometry ground truth:

> A. Geiger, P. Lenz, C. Stiller, R. Urtasun. "Vision meets Robotics: The KITTI
> Dataset." International Journal of Robotics Research, 2013.
> https://www.cvlibs.net/datasets/kitti/

Contents per sequence (generated by `atlas_kitti export-replay` and
`tools/export_replay.py`; formats in docs/replay.md):

- `map.bin`: Atlas's map, built from Atlas's own estimated poses, one point per
  0.5 m cube (int16 decimeters).
- `scans.bin`: every laser scan, thinned to 640 points (int16 centimeters).
- `frames.json`: per-frame poses, errors against the ground truth, and the
  engine's diagnostics. Includes ground-truth positions from KITTI.
